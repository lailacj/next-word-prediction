James A. Michaelov, Benjamin K. Bergen,
Better language models better model the N400, but not reading time,
Journal of Memory and Language,

Paper abstract:
“Based on an analysis of 4 reading time datasets and 9 N400 datasets, we replicate past results for reading time, but find that larger language models, models that are trained on more data, and models that perform better at next-word prediction and other more complex natural language tasks are better able to predict N400 amplitude. We interpret this difference between the N400 and reading time measures as potentially revealing the comparative importance of semantic prediction in the neurocognitive processes indexed by the N400.”
